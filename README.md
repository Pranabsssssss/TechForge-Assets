# TechForge Assets

> Official assets website for **TechForge**, my team during the 21-day AI + Robotics bootcamp at IIIT-Delhi.

## About

**TechForge Assets** is the dedicated assets website created for **Team TechForge** during the intensive **21-day AI + Robotics bootcamp at IIIT-Delhi**.

The website was created to organize, present, and manage the digital assets associated with our team and robotics project throughout the bootcamp.

This repository is primarily an **assets and presentation website**, rather than the main robotics project itself.

---

# TechForge

**TechForge** was the team name used during the 21-day AI + Robotics bootcamp.

The team worked together throughout the practical phase of the program, combining:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Robotics
- Software
- Hardware
- Electronics
- Mechanical Design
- CAD
- Computer Vision
- Embedded Systems

The assets website served as a centralized place for the team's visual and project-related resources.

---

# Bootcamp Context

The project was created during the final practical stage of a larger selection process.

The overall program involved **75,000+ students**, with participants progressively filtered through multiple stages of written examinations and selection rounds.

The final shortlisted participants entered an intensive **21-day practical AI + Robotics bootcamp at IIIT-Delhi**.
